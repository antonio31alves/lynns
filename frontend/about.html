Our story

Travel shaped by family, experience and local knowledge.

Lynn's Travel has grown from a small Eastbourne travel business into a local coach-travel team serving communities across East Sussex.

Our roots

1992

The year Lynn Offord started the business, first operating as Lynn's Department within Fairdeal Travel at Langney Shopping Centre.

From Langney to Eastbourne town centre

The business moved into Eastbourne town-centre premises in 2000. Over time, the area served expanded beyond Eastbourne to include Seaford, Bexhill, Heathfield and Uckfield.

Today, Lynn's Travel focuses on coach holidays and short breaks, day excursions and private coach hire. The family team brings first-hand experience of coach operations and the places travellers visit.

  1. 1992

    Lynn begins the travel business in Langney.

  2. 2000
